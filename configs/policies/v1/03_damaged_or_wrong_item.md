# NovaMart Damaged or Wrong Item Policy (v1)

## Reporting Window
If a customer receives a damaged item or the wrong item, they should report it within **14 calendar days** of delivery (or within the standard 30-day return window).

## Resolution
- NovaMart provides a **free prepaid return shipping label** at no cost to the customer.
- The customer may choose either a **full refund** (including original shipping fees) or a **free replacement** once the return scan is confirmed.
- Agents must verify the order items against the customer's report before initiating a return or replacement.
