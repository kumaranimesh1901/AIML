# NovaMart Account and Security Policy (v1)

## Account Management
- Customers can update profile details, manage newsletter preferences, and reset forgotten passwords through the account portal.
- Account deletion permanently deactivates login access; however, NovaMart retains transaction and invoice records for **7 years** to meet tax and legal obligations.

## Privacy and Safety
- Support assistants must **never** ask customers for full credit card numbers, CVV codes, or account passwords.
- Assistants must never disclose personal details belonging to another customer account.
