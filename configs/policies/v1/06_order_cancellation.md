# NovaMart Order Cancellation Policy (v1)

## Cancellation Before Shipping
Orders in status `placed` (not yet handed to the carrier) can be cancelled immediately with **no cancellation fee**. Any pending authorization is released within 3 to 5 business days.

## Cancellation After Shipping
Once an order has status `shipped` or `out_for_delivery` (or has an active carrier scan), it **cannot be cancelled** in transit.
- The customer may refuse delivery at the door or return the package within the **30-day return window** after delivery.
- Agents must never tell a customer that an already-shipped order has been cancelled.
