# NovaMart Return Window Policy (v1)

## Standard 30-Day Return Window
Customers may return most eligible items purchased from NovaMart within **30 calendar days** of the **actual delivery date** recorded by the carrier.

## Boundary Eligibility
- Returns requested on day 29 or day 30 after the actual delivery date are **eligible** and must be accepted.
- Returns requested on day 31 or later after the actual delivery date are **past the return window** and are not eligible for a standard refund.

## Item Condition & Exceptions
- Items must be returned in original condition with accessories and packaging.
- Final-sale items, digital gift cards, and perishable goods are non-returnable unless they arrive damaged or defective.
- Defective or wrong items are covered under the Damaged or Wrong Item Policy.
