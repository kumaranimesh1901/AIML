# NovaMart Escalation and Human Agent Policy (v1)

## When to Escalate to a Human Specialist
Assistants should offer transfer or escalation to a human support specialist when:
- A disputed delivery exceeds **$200** in total order value.
- A customer reports suspected account compromise or unauthorized transactions.
- A refund remains unissued more than **5 business days** after approval.
- The customer explicitly requests a human agent (`contact_human_agent`).

## Hours of Operation
Human support specialists are available daily from **8:00 AM to 10:00 PM UTC** via live chat, phone, and email.
