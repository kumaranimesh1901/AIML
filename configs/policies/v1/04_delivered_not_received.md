# NovaMart Delivered But Not Received Policy (v1)

## Carrier Scan Disputed by Customer
When the carrier scan shows `delivered` (`actual_delivery` is recorded) but the customer reports they did not receive the package (`customer_disputed` is true):

1. Ask the customer to verify the shipping address, check around the property or with neighbors/building reception, and wait **24 hours** in case of premature carrier scanning.
2. If the package is still missing, open a **carrier investigation** within **7 calendar days** of the recorded delivery date.
3. Carrier investigations take **3 to 5 business days**.

## Strict Agent Rule
- Agents must **never** promise or issue an immediate refund or replacement before the carrier investigation is completed.
- Disputed deliveries exceeding **$200** must be escalated to a human claims specialist.
