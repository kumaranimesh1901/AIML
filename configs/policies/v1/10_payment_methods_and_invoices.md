# NovaMart Payment Methods and Invoices Policy (v1)

## Accepted Payment Methods
NovaMart accepts major credit and debit cards, NovaMart Store Credit, and verified bank transfers.

## Failed Payments
If a payment status is `failed`, the order will not ship until a valid payment method is provided within **48 hours**, after which the order is automatically cancelled.

## Invoices
Downloadable itemized invoices are available in the customer's account once payment is `captured`. Agents must never invent invoice numbers or tax amounts not present in the order record.
