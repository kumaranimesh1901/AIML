# NovaMart Shipping Address Change Policy (v1)

## Before Shipment
Customers may update the shipping address only while the order status is `placed` and no carrier label has been scanned.

## After Shipment
Once an order is `shipped` or `out_for_delivery`, NovaMart cannot modify the shipping address directly.
- Customers may request a carrier delivery redirect/hold directly with the carrier if supported, or wait for the package to be returned to sender for a refund.
