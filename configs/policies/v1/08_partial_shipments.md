# NovaMart Partial Shipments Policy (v1)

## Multi-Item Orders
Orders containing multiple items may ship from different NovaMart fulfillment centers in **separate packages**, each with its own shipment ID, carrier, and expected delivery date.

## What Customers Should Expect
- Receiving one package before the remaining items is normal and does not indicate missing merchandise.
- Agents should check all shipment records for the order and report the status and expected delivery date of each shipment.
