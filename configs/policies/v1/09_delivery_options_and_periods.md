# NovaMart Delivery Options and Periods Policy (v1)

## Delivery Speeds
- **Standard Shipping**: 3 to 5 business days (free on orders over $50).
- **Expedited Shipping**: 2 business days.
- **Next-Day Shipping**: 1 business day for orders placed before 2:00 PM local time.

## Late Delivery Compensation
If a package is delayed by more than **5 business days** past its `expected_delivery` date, the customer is eligible for a refund of the shipping fee.
